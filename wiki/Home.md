# Notch wiki

The long form behind the notch's screens: what each label does, why a state reads the way it does, and what to try when something is off. The notch itself stays quiet; a one-line tip appears under a row when you rest the pointer on it or walk to it with the arrow keys, and everything longer lives here.

- [Getting started](Getting-started.md): install, the first minute, going back to another bar.
- [Settings](Settings.md): every row of the panel, the header buttons, and the keys that have no row.
- [Plugins](Plugins.md): installing, updating and removing Face ID, Profiles and OLED Corners, and the notch's own updates.
- [Setup points](Setup-points.md): what the Setup page checks, what Fix changes, and Snapshots.
- [Integrations](Integrations.md): plugin panels drawn inside the notch.
- [Notifications](Notifications.md): toasts in the notch, the volume and brightness card, and the notification light.
- [Menu](Menu.md): Omarchy's menu drawn inside the notch.
- [Lock and Face](Lock-and-Face.md): Face ID, the lock screen, and the rename from `graveklar.face`.
- [Profiles](Profiles.md): the Profiles plugin and how the notch hosts it.
- [Corners](Corners.md): the OLED Corners plugin and its settings panel.
- [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md): what each message means and what to do about it.
- [Export and import](Export-and-import.md): sharing your settings as a code or a file. Planned; not in the notch yet.

For the code, the plugin contract and how the shape and glow are drawn, read the [README](../README.md) and [SURFACE-HOSTING.md](../SURFACE-HOSTING.md).
